# NATO Squad: Privacy Policy

The hosted version of this policy is at https://sites.google.com/view/og-software/privacy-policy. Keep the two in step.

**NATO Squad** is made by **OG Software**.

## What the game stores
- Your progress is kept on your device, in the game's private storage. It includes gold, upgrades, modules,
  mission stars and settings.
- If you're signed in to **Game Center** (iPhone and iPad) or **Google Play Games** (Android), a copy of your progress
  is also kept in your Game Center / Play Games saved games (on iPhone this uses your iCloud Drive), so it comes back
  if you change devices or reinstall. Your Game Center / Play Games nickname and scores (enemies destroyed, gold
  earned) appear on the game's leaderboards, where other players can see them, and your achievements are recorded
  there. This is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; OG Software doesn't receive your Apple
  or Google account details.
- The game has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- The game does not sell personal information.

## Purchases
- In-app purchases are handled entirely by the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play**. OG Software never receives your
  payment details.
- The game keeps a list of completed purchase transaction IDs on your device so that a purchase is never delivered
  twice. Purchase records are subject to Apple's or Google's own privacy policies.

## Children
NATO Squad does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data
- **Settings → Reset Progress** deletes all game data on the device and replaces the Game Center / Play Games copy
  with the empty profile.
- Achievements and leaderboard scores are kept by Apple or Google; you can manage them, or sign out, in Game Center
  or Play Games.
