# NATO Squad: Privacy Policy

The hosted version of this policy is at https://sites.google.com/view/og-software/privacy-policy. Keep the two in step.

**NATO Squad** is made by **OG Software**.

## What the game stores
- Your progress is kept **only on your device**, in the game's private storage. It includes gold, upgrades, modules,
  mission stars and settings.
- The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- The game does not collect, send or sell personal information.

## Purchases
- In-app purchases are handled entirely by the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play**. OG Software never receives your
  payment details.
- The game keeps a list of completed purchase transaction IDs on your device so that a purchase is never delivered
  twice. Purchase records are subject to Apple's or Google's own privacy policies.

## Children
NATO Squad does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data
- **Settings → Reset Progress** deletes all game data on the device.
- Uninstalling the game also deletes it.
